## 摘要
大气表面层流动在计算上具有挑战性，主要归因于地表粗糙度和高雷诺数，二者都要求近地表区域具有极高的空间分辨率。我们开发了一种基于二维随机的模型，用于生成并沿流向拼接瞬时阶跃型速度剖面，这些剖面具有壁湍流的关键要素，即均匀动量区（UMZ）和剪切层（Ehsani et al. 2024a,b），以及涡旋（Ehsani et al. 2026）。本文将该模型扩展至对数层顶部，以复现 Saddoughi 和 Veeravalli（2000）测量的高雷诺数粗糙壁面湍流边界层；该扩展无需 UMZ 数据集支持，仅使用少量关键流动参数：泰勒微尺度 λT、边界层高度 δ、摩擦速度 uτ 和气动粗糙度长度 z0。主要挑战在于将随机模型与 UMZ 及涡旋特征的缩放分布进行整合。所得统计矩、能谱和结构函数与实验结果进行了比较。经验证的代码已在 GitHub 仓库中公开。

## Abstract
Atmospheric surface layer flows are computationally challenging, predominantly due to surface roughness and the high Reynolds number, both of which demand exceptionally high spatial resolution in the near-surface region. We have developed a 2-D stochastic-based model for the generation and the streamwise concatenation of instantaneous, step-like velocity profiles featuring key elements of wall turbulent flows, i.e., uniform momentum zones (UMZ) and shear layers (Ehsani et al. 2024a,b), and vortices (Ehsani et al. 2026). The model is extended herein to the top of the logarithmic layer to reproduce the high-Reynolds-number, rough-wall, turbulent boundary layer measured by Saddoughi and Veeravalli(2000), without the support of a UMZ dataset, using only a handful of critical flow parameters: the Taylor microscale λT, the boundary layer height δ, the friction velocity uτ, and the aerodynamic roughness length z0. The primary challenge lies in the integration of the stochastic model with the scaled distributions of the UMZ and vortex characteristics. The resulting statistical moments, energy spectra, and structure functions are compared against the experimental results. The validated code is made available in a GitHub repository.
