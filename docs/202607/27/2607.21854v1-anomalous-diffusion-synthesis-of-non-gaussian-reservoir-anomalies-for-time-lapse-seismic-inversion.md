## 摘要
我们开发了一个物理信息框架，用于学习型时移（四维）地震反演，该反演估计与地下流体迁移相关的生产引起的速度变化。现有的学习型反演方法通常基于高斯扩散模型生成的地震数据进行训练，这些模型未能捕捉非均质储层中的反常输运特征。为克服这一局限，我们引入了一个基于时空分数阶扩散的数据生成器，其中时间和空间分数阶分别独立控制记忆效应和非局部输运，而椭圆各向异性则代表优势流。通过采样该参数空间，我们生成了一个涵盖正常、次扩散、超扩散和中间输运机制的广泛且物理一致的摄动速度场集成。随后，我们训练了一个卷积神经网络，直接将时移地震残差映射到速度更新。通过广泛的蒙特卡洛验证，我们展示了该网络能够准确重建所有输运机制下的生产引起的异常体，且重建误差主要由异常振幅和界面清晰度控制，而非扩散机制本身。我们的结果表明，将反常输运物理嵌入训练分布中，显著扩展了摊销四维地震反演的适用性，使其能够在现有方法所依赖的高斯假设之外，稳健地恢复重尾和各向异性的储层特征。

## Abstract
We develop a physics-informed framework for learned time-lapse (4D) seismic inversion that estimates production-induced velocity changes associated with subsurface fluid migration. Existing learned inversion methods are commonly trained on seismic data generated from Gaussian diffusion models, which fail to capture the anomalous transport characteristic of heterogeneous subsurface reservoirs. To overcome this limitation, we introduce a data generator based on space-time fractional diffusion, in which the temporal and spatial fractional orders independently control memory effects and nonlocal transport, while elliptical anisotropy represents preferential flow. By sampling this parameter space, we generate a broad ensemble of physically consistent velocity perturbations spanning normal, subdiffusive, superdiffusive, and intermediate transport regimes. We then train a convolutional neural network to map time-lapse seismic residuals directly to velocity updates. Through extensive Monte Carlo validation, we show that the network accurately reconstructs production-induced anomalies across all transport regimes, with reconstruction errors governed primarily by anomaly amplitude and interface sharpness rather than by the diffusion regime itself. Our results demonstrate that embedding anomalous-transport physics into the training distribution substantially extends the applicability of amortized 4D seismic inversion, enabling robust recovery of heavy-tailed and anisotropic reservoir signatures beyond the Gaussian assumptions underlying existing approaches.
